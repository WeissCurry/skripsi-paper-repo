# 1. Introduction

Blockchain technology has transformed the global financial landscape, yet in Indonesia the adoption of Ethereum *staking* faces a twofold challenge: technical-financial risk and uncertainty over Sharia compliance. According to the Fatwa of the Majelis Tarjih of PP Muhammadiyah (2026), crypto assets are recognized as *Māl Mutaqawwam*, but *yield generation* practices that contain an element of interest remain prohibited.

The core problem in conventional *Liquid Staking* protocols (such as Lido) is the use of the **Rebasing** algorithm. This mechanism automatically increases the user's token balance without any underlying exchange of value (<Glossary term="Underlying Asset" definition="The base asset that backs the value of a financial instrument or digital derivative.">underlying asset exchange</Glossary>), which substantially resembles the practice of **Riba Nasi'ah**. In addition, distributing penalties (*socialized penalty*) without regard to individual fault can give rise to **<Glossary term="Zulm" definition="Injustice or oppression; here, the disproportionate distribution of slashing penalties to blameless investors.">Zulm</Glossary>** (injustice).

# 2. Methodology: TOGAF ADM

This study adopts the **TOGAF ADM** framework (Preliminary through Phase D) to align strategic business objectives with the technology infrastructure.

![TOGAF ADM Framework](/content/proposal-images/image18.png)
*Figure: Applying the TOGAF ADM cycle in system design*

1.  **Preliminary Phase**: Establishes the principle of *Hifzul Mal* (protection of wealth) and data transparency.
2.  **Phase A (Architecture Vision)**: Sets the vision of an integrated risk-management system.
3.  **Phase B (Business Architecture)**: Redesigns the staking workflow from the *Rebase* model to the *NAV* model.
4.  **Phase C (Information Systems Architecture)**: Defines the smart contract data structure and application components.
5.  **Phase D (Technology Architecture)**: Determines the validator node infrastructure and cloud environment.

# 3. Proposed Architecture: The NAV Model

## 3.1 Business Architecture (Phase B)
The main proposed solution is the use of a **Net Asset Value (NAV)** model, much like a Sharia mutual-fund instrument.

![BPMN NAV Initiation](/content/proposal-images/image35.png)
*Figure: BPMN of the NAV- and Wakalah-contract-based initiation phase*

In this model:
*   **LST issuance**: The protocol computes the pool NAV at deposit time and mints a proof-of-ownership token (*Shares*).
*   **Return distribution**: Rewards or penalties (*slashing*) from the Ethereum network accumulate into the pool, which automatically raises or lowers the token's exchange rate (NAV) **rather than increasing the token count**.

![BPMN Reward & Penalty](/content/proposal-images/image36.png)
*Figure: Organic return-distribution mechanism that eliminates Riba*

# 4. Risk Management & Shariah Compliance

## 4.1 Assessment Parameters & Veto Right
The system uses a **Risk & Compliance Scoring Engine** to evaluate validators against 10 indicators.

## 4.2 Attack Mitigation & Asset Protection
*   **Emergency Pause**: If the *Scoring Engine* detects an anomaly in *Client Diversity* (centralization threat), the system automatically locks the *Vault* to protect assets.
*   **<Glossary term="Inflation Attack" definition="An attack on a pool smart contract in which the attacker manipulates the exchange-rate ratio with a small early deposit to steal subsequent investors' funds.">Inflation Attack</Glossary> Mitigation**: A *decimal offset* mechanism prevents an attacker from manipulating the initial deposit, ensuring fairness for retail investors.

# 6. Conclusion

The proposed architecture with the **Net Asset Value (NAV)** model proves fundamentally capable of eliminating the critical points of **Riba** and **Zulm**. By replacing balance manipulation with organic growth in asset valuation, blockchain innovation can now run in harmony with Sharia principles. Using TOGAF ADM ensures that every technical aspect of the system stays within the corridor of wealth protection (*Hifzul Mal*) and fairness for all stakeholders.
